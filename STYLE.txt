GARY EATS! — VISUAL, CAMERA AND ASSET CONTRACT
Revision: 15 October 2026 | Applies to live game and planned chapters.
Read PROJECT_STATE.md and the live game-data.js / gary-game-config.json first.
This document is a visual contract, NOT a second source of numeric calibration.

1. IDENTITY AND ART DIRECTION
Painted British high-street cartoon; clean dark outlines, restrained texture,
soft material shading and convincing but modest depth. Gary is the most
recognisable, relatively detailed character: exact face/hair, blue quilted
jacket/gilet, blue jumper, collar, trousers, shoes and chest microphone.
Artwork must preserve character identity between close-up and tiny runner sizes.
Do not switch into unrelated photorealism, anime proportions, pixel art,
stock-game icons or dramatic isometric rendering. Keep expressive humour and
warmth as the narrative environment becomes darker.

2. CAMERA, WORLD AND SCALE
The runner is a 360 x 640 design canvas; high-DPI phone backing pixels do not
change authoring coordinates. Fixed camera, parallel horizontal lane positions;
NO receding vanishing point, simulated lens or distance-based object shrinkage.
Road x = 105..255, width 150. Three 50-unit lanes, centres 130 / 180 / 230.
Gary's world reference is y = 540, visible sprite target height 142; feet are
anchored using WORLD.playerFootOffset = 6.
Shops are painted near-front elevations with only a little roof/top depth,
long wall parallel to the road, facade oriented towards the road. Destination
size 89 x 236: left shop at x=0, right shop at x=271. Adjacent pavement strips
x=89..105 and 255..271. Street segment height 360; detail zone height 124.
The source shop atlas is 2912 x 2688; respect individually authored source crops.
Items use ITEMS.displaySize separately from ITEMS.collision. Present settings:
kebab/fish 50, roast 46, mushroom 52, fork 44 design pixels.
Props must share the shop-camera language, have plausible ground contact and
stay legible at their authored DETAILS display caps. No floating benches,
flat sticker-like trees or props that look attached to the building wall.

3. COBBLE GROUND AND TILE SEAMS
Current renderer uses WORLD.cobbleCell = 48 design units. drawCobbles() samples
ONE QUADRANT of each corresponding WebP texture into each destination cell.
This is not a 180/200-unit full-texture draw. All eight variants must share the
same authored source size/quadrant layout, colour family, scale and edge language.
Use one scrolling world-space grid for left and right; do not restart the pattern
at shop or detail-zone boundaries. All cells must align without pixel gaps,
white seams or a change in phase as the street moves.
The 60-unit suggestion is a visual experiment, NOT published as of this review.
A larger grid makes existing details appear larger but cannot guarantee
seamlessness if textures do not tile at their quadrant edges. Inspect source
art and gameplay captures before making a more intrusive change. Contrast,
stone boundaries, weeds and cracks must be distinguishable on iPhone without
turning every variant into a sharply contrasting patchwork.

4. SHOPKEEPERS, GLASS AND SHUTTERS — INDEPENDENT CONTROLS
Each of the six shops has different, already-published rectangles and offsets.
DO NOT replace those with one universal measurement or the original defaults.
- GOLD: gary-game-config.json -> shopKeeperAnchors[shop]; independent keeper
  base rectangle (x/y/w/h). Editing this MUST NOT move the blue glow region.
- Keeper controls: shopKeepers[shop] offsets, scale, idle/cheer amounts.
- GREEN: shopKeepers[shop].clip is the visibility window/mask, not an anchor.
- BLUE: shopWindowPanes[shop] defines the illuminated glass and window sheen.
  Editing BLUE MUST NOT resize or relocate the independently anchored keeper.
- AMBER: shopClosures[shop].shutter and optional doorLight; independent closing
  rectangles, duration and approach timing.
All are local 89 x 236 design coordinates. game-data.js SHOP_PANES fractions
are fallback geometry only; the published JSON supersedes them. Do not quote
old calibration coordinates as the six shops' current exact positions.
Glass art, keeper motion, night lighting and shutters must stay spatially
consistent. Keepers draw ABOVE night-window/storm glow and BELOW foreground
shutters; avoid a later glow pass that fades the keeper. Minimise sheen over
the face. Baked keepers within source shop images are a separate art concern;
do not mask a double-figure problem by moving the live character incorrectly.
Shops: Fish & Chips, Peri Peri Chicken, Bakery, The Red Lion, Butchers,
Mallace Cafe. Identity and readable signage are non-negotiable.

5. ANIMATION, CELLS, ALPHA AND HANDOFFS
World sprites require a real alpha channel (PNG/WebP); no JPEG backdrops,
false checkerboards, stray light fringe, baked sky/road or per-frame trimming.
All frames of one sprite share the same untrimmed cell dimensions, subject
scale and alignment. Keep arms, shoes and microphone within safe borders.
- Talking intro: gary_intro_sheet.PNG, 4 x 2 cells of 384 x 512, 1536 x 1024.
- Running/walking: gary_walk_sheet.PNG, 6 equal horizontal frames, one row.
  Frame width is source width / 6; keep one stable bottom-centre foot anchor.
- Cinematic transformation: gary_transition_sheet.png, 4 x 1 cells of
  384 x 512, overall 1536 x 512. It must link the last talking pose to the
  first walking pose. JavaScript owns motion/scale/crossfade; do not bake
  changing character scale or camera travel into these four image cells.
- Shop sprite atlas: high_street_sprites.webp, 2912 x 2688; honour published
  atlas source coordinates. Prop strips may use separate frame counts.
- Other loop/one-off pictures: use current GARY_SPRITES definitions as source.
Animation acceptance requires playback at in-game size: check for costume,
proportion or head-direction pops; bouncing baselines; frame bleeding;
wrong source slicing; and unnatural transitions. A still image is insufficient.

6. LAYER ORDER AND WEATHER
Day: soft light from upper left, restrained shadows, clear outlines.
Night: cool environment shade plus warm amber window/lamp light. Essential
food pickups and hazards always read clearly. Do not bake a night version of
all source art; use code lighting and separate glow sprites/passes instead.
Target compositing order:
  world floor -> shops/props -> ambient/night treatment -> local glass and
  window light/storm glow -> keepers (independently clipped) -> shutters and
  appropriate foreground props -> gameplay actors/items -> restrained rain
  streaks -> edge vignette -> HUD/dialogue.
Actual implementation may split passes to keep the lighting behind keepers;
review order in CURRENT active engine whenever it changes.
Rain streaks are thin, reusable, bounded, and must not obscure lane reading.
Cobble splashes are small flattened ground marks that scroll with the floor.
Rain effects are cosmetic; never use rain opacity to conceal lethal hazards.
UI/cutscenes remain above the weather. Avoid full-screen shake or flashes.

7. UI, FOOD DISCOVERY AND FUTURE USP
Main menu must offer easy Play Game and Food Catalogue actions on a phone.
Discovered-food art, labels and collection counts must be readable; unknown
foods are deliberately locked without spoiling the identity. Persistent
progress belongs to an explicit save policy (currently same-device localStorage).
A future Review Effect or combination mechanic should use a consistent visual
language with memorable character reactions; it must not be mistaken for a
shipped cooking system or obscure lane inputs. Distinguish food from hazards
by shape first, not only by hue. Keep touch targets large enough for iPhone.

8. NARRATIVE ASSET RULES (PLANNED, NOT ACTIVE)
The Last Orders stranger: elderly former chip-shop owner, raincoat, dry paper
bag; quiet partly obscured face, coherent between world and portrait art.
A fixed-camera sidewalk approach needs stable ground contact and separate
portrait framing if text dialogue requires face detail. Lantern: battered
warm-amber body and legible UI silhouette, light composited separately. The
light may reveal optional bonus food, not remove hazards or make lanes readable.
Wilderness must retain 3-lane geometry and clear safe space while familiar
shops give way to vegetation and watchful eyes outside the road.
All of these require dedicated implementations and asset review; they remain
future concepts in DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.

9. ACCEPTANCE AND REGRESSION GATE
Each new/modified asset: exact business/character; same camera; source size
and per-frame bounding cells; true alpha; world display size; ground anchor;
lighting; clip; contact; layer; safety; iPhone-scale readability. Record
PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIED. Compare on contrasting backgrounds and in DAY,
NIGHT and HEAVY RAIN; check moving video/screenshots for frame continuity.
Make only tested changes to live source. After new images/scripts, update the
appropriate ?v= cache reference and retest Play/Skip/collection/restart.
ASSET_GRID.json/png are historical review snapshots. The current repository
and actual rendered game supersede them where they disagree.
