Gary Eats art lock. Every new picture is checked against this before it is used.

Camera. Not a vanishing-point street. Lanes are flat bands. Shops are front-on with a little of the top showing. Props use that same camera. Gary is from behind, head turned so the face is visible. One ground line. One light, soft daylight, from the same side.

Style. Painted British cartoon. Clean dark outlines. Semi-realistic, not cute, not pixel, not photo. Same face, hair, blue quilted gilet, blue knit jumper, mic with a fuzzy windscreen. No black fringe, no white border, no drop shadow in the picture. Cut out on flat black.

Scale, Gary is the ruler. A plate is about a third of his height. A hazard is no taller than a plate. A bin, postbox, bench or board is between his knee and his chest. A tree is about chest height. A shop is taller than him and the window is not resized.

Reject a picture that is the right style and the wrong angle, or the right angle and a different outline. Pack into a sheet only after the set is agreed.
