THE NEXT EXPERIENCE
After a sustained nighttime run, heavy rain builds. The street becomes very dark,
shops disappear, and Gary slows down. A shadowy former chip-shop owner approaches
on the pavement. Gameplay pauses for their first text encounter. He gives Gary
The Last Orders Lantern, which later reveals hidden bonus-food trails.
Chapter 5 leads into wilderness, trees and eyes beyond the road.
