GARY EATS! — ASSET REVIEW / GENERATION ACCEPTANCE PROMPT
Revision: 15 October 2026
Purpose: examine actual assets AND their in-game rendering. Not permission to
redesign or publish them automatically. Source of truth is game-data.js,
gary-game-config.json, STYLE.txt and the actively loaded game engine.

SUPPLY TO THE REVIEWER
- The approved gameplay screenshot at native 360x640 design scale, with current
  daylight and night/rain references where the object will actually appear.
- Current STYLE.txt, relevant game-data.js definitions, the current
  gary-game-config.json shop entries and (if animated) real sprite-sheet PNG.
- Source asset, requested change, exact source and destination dimensions,
  collision/visibility/camera requirements, and current file/path.
- ASSET_GRID.json/png may be added as historical references, but are NOT proof
  of current sprite source or game layout. Disclose outdated metadata.
- If evidence is missing, explicitly mark the assessment UNVERIFIED.

REVIEW SEQUENCE (BLOCKING FAULTS FIRST)
1. IDENTITY — Exact intended food/shop/character, spelling/signage, hairstyle,
   facial structure, Gary outfit, microphone and visual continuity.
2. CAMERA — Parallel lane/world angle, barely elevated near-front shop facade,
   no isometric angle, dramatic perspective, foreshortened runners or random
   object rotations. Use existing approved world crop, not a new camera guess.
3. PIXEL GEOMETRY — Source bounds, destination scale, per-shop 89x236 footprint,
   atlas crops, no cut-off shoes/heads, coherent prop size and collision clarity.
4. GROUNDING — Contact shadows and bottom anchor. Avoid floating benches,
   objects pinned to walls, sliding keepers or footprints moving with frame crop.
5. SPRITE ALPHA — Real transparency and clean fringe on BLACK, WHITE and
   MID-TONE backdrops. A JPEG image or white checkerboard is not alpha.
6. ANIMATION — Inspect ALL frames, equal cell dimensions, untrimmed alignment,
   full-motion playback, exact first-to-last transition, no flickering costumes.
   Check the intro -> 4-frame cinematic -> first walking frame handoff. No
   baked shrinking, camera motion, or blurred frame transitions.
7. SHOPS — Compare actual published per-shop GOLD/BLUE/GREEN/AMBER rectangles
   and their relative layers. GOLD reposition does not change BLUE lighting.
   GREEN only clips the keeper. AMBER shutters go over the correctly lit keeper.
8. COBBLES — Inspect each of eight variants, not merely one screenshot. Does
   the stone edge align across all 4 source quadrants and across changing
   material types? Does the floor remain one scrolling grid at 48-unit cells?
   Small weeds, cracks or leaf shapes should be clear but not repeated visibly
   in a distracting pattern. If testing 60 units, treat as a proposed preview.
9. MATERIAL/LIGHT — Same painted British-cartoon material response. Day upper-
   left light; cool nighttime treatment; warmly lit windows that do NOT wash
   out the keeper. Check storm darkness, lamp halo, wet cobble reflections.
10. GAMEPLAY — Hazards and pickups must remain distinguishable on iPhone in
    daylight and rain. Verify collision appearance and safe-lane readability.
    Props and effects do not block touch targets, dialogue or HUD.
11. UI/FOOD — Verify Food Catalogue cards both undiscovered and unlocked;
    imagery, names, rarity (if designed) and counts are readable. Recipe
    combination/crafting art is FUTURE concept, not active system.
12. STORY — Future stranger must have one identity across world/portrait,
    grounded feet, limited face reveal, legible lantern silhouette; dialogue
    fallback works even if optional art fails. Do not approve unimplemented
    scenes as if demonstrated in the playable game.

SPECIAL REJECTION CHECKS
- A two-lane roadblock must actually span 2 adjacent 50-unit lanes and leave
  one readable safe lane; collider/pattern changes are separately required.
- Generic 'British shop' art is NOT a substitute for Fish & Chips, Bakery,
  Peri Peri Chicken, Butchers, The Red Lion, or Mallace Cafe.
- For modified shops, protect the live window/door/glass coordinates; when an
  artist cannot retain them, stop for explicit re-calibration approval.
- New cobble textures must use compatible quadrant edges at the authored
  source size. Merely repeating an AI image does not make it seamless.
- Rain splash loops should be small, ground-bound and non-hazard-looking.
- Transition artwork should be judged playing, not by a static 4-up sheet.
- Never infer that an asset file is live merely because it exists in GitHub.
  Confirm actual game-data.js/index.html/module reference and on-phone render.

REPORT FORMAT
Provide a table with the following columns:
ID | actual repo source | exact display geometry | PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED |
evidence observed | exact fault | smallest safe correction | next phone test

Below the table, separate BLOCKING issues (wrong identity/camera, broken alpha,
source-cell geometry, clipping, wrong gold/blue coupling, illegible hazards,
missing asset/script) from POLISH issues (subtle texture, expression timing,
slightly excessive saturation). State what was checked in code, visually,
with animation playback, and on the live phone, respectively.

ACCEPTANCE PRINCIPLE
One correct-looking frame is not game-ready. Only approve after visual,
geometry, transparency, runtime-scale, animation and mobile readability checks.
A failed/unknown check is UNVERIFIED, not implied success. Changes to engine
or live configuration require staging, regression tests and a rollback.
